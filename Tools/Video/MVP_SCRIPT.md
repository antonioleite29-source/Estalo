# MVP demo video — shot list and narration

Target length **1:45**, which fits Tom's "one to two minutes". Portuguese narration, Portuguese on
screen. Two phones needed for the duel section; a third pair of hands helps.

Record the phone screen rather than filming it: on Android, Settings > Quick settings > Screen
record, at the highest quality offered. Turn on Do Not Disturb first. Record each shot separately
and cut them together — one continuous take will show every loading pause.

Narrate after the edit, over the finished cut. Read slowly; the figures below assume about 150 words
a minute in Portuguese.

---

## 0:00–0:12 — Opening

**Picture.** The launch screen, then the lobby with the IQ bar and Jogar. Hold on the bar.

**Narration.**
> Estalo é um duelo de matemática em tempo real. Duas pessoas, a mesma pergunta, ao mesmo tempo —
> quem acertar primeiro marca o ponto.

**On screen.** Title card: **ESTALO — Acerte primeiro.**

---

## 0:12–0:30 — The tutorial

**Picture.** First launch on a clean install, so the tutorial plays. Show two cards, then the test
round, then the result screen with the starting IQ.

**Narration.**
> Quem abre o jogo pela primeira vez aprende jogando. Sete perguntas definem o nível inicial, de um
> a sete, e a partir daí as perguntas se ajustam a quem está jogando.

**Cut tightly.** Two cards is enough; the full tutorial runs longer than the whole video.

---

## 0:30–1:00 — The duel, both phones

**Picture.** Two phones side by side, filmed together, or two screen recordings cut side by side.
Press Jogar on both. Show the match starting, a question, one player answering first, the score
going up.

Then the key moment: one player gets it wrong, the ring starts, and the other has four seconds alone.

**Narration.**
> A partida é contra outra pessoa de verdade, no mesmo nível que você. E tem uma regra que muda
> tudo: errou, o adversário ganha quatro segundos para responder sozinho. Responder rápido demais
> custa caro. Pensar demais também.

**On screen.** Label the moment: **Errou? O outro responde sozinho.**

---

## 1:00–1:20 — Learning path and practice

**Picture.** Aprender tab. Scroll the trail, open a lesson, show a teaching card, then two questions
and the counter going up. Then the Profile page with the stats filled in.

**Narration.**
> Fora das partidas, há uma trilha de vinte lições, da adição às equações. Cada lição ensina
> primeiro e cobra depois: dez acertos para concluir. E o jogo guarda os assuntos em que você mais
> erra, para treinar só neles.

---

## 1:20–1:35 — Scale and account

**Picture.** The Profile page stats: partidas, vitórias, pontos, ranking global. Then the login
panel, typed quickly, and the account line afterwards.

**Narration.**
> Tudo fica salvo em um servidor próprio: pontuação, histórico e o ranking de todo mundo que joga.
> Uma conta leva menos de um minuto e leva o progresso para qualquer celular.

---

## 1:35–1:45 — Close

**Picture.** Back to the lobby, the IQ bar, then the title card.

**Narration.**
> Estalo. Feito para quem acha que matemática fica melhor quando é uma disputa com os amigos.

**On screen.** **ESTALO** · em breve no Google Play.

---

## Checklist before recording

- Install the newest APK on both phones, and check the server is up.
- Clear the app data on one phone so the first-run tutorial plays.
- Set both display names to something short; long names wrap on the board.
- Pick avatars that differ, so the two sides read apart on camera.
- Battery full, Do Not Disturb on, notifications off.
- Record in portrait, 1080 x 1920 or better.

## Editing notes

- Cut every loading screen and every wait for matchmaking. The video should never sit still.
- Keep the game's own sound under the narration; the point sound and the win jingle carry the moment.
- Portuguese subtitles for the narration: the room where it gets presented is not always quiet.
- No music under the narration beyond the game's own, so nothing competes with the voice.
