# Music shortlist

Six candidates for the lobby loop, three each for the match loop, the win sting and the lose sting.
Download, rename to `Lobby`, `Match`, `Win` or `Lose`, drop in `Assets/Resources/Music`. Import
settings and the rest are in `MUSIC.md`.

Two things to know before picking:

- **Attribution column.** "No" means nothing to do. "Yes (CC BY)" means the credit line must go in
  the Créditos panel on the More page, or you are breaking the licence.
- Where a row points at a search page instead of one track, it is because the catalogue changes and
  a direct link goes stale — the search is the stable address. Open it, listen to the first few,
  take the one that fits.

## Checks before shipping

- Read the licence on each download page yourself. Some Pixabay entries are credited to
  `freesound_community` and can carry different terms from the Pixabay Content License.
- Tracks older than 2019 on Pixabay were uploaded under CC0, newer ones under the Pixabay Content
  License: both allow use in a paid-listing app, neither allows reselling the file on its own.
- FreePD was suggested earlier in `MUSIC.md`; a Wikimedia Commons note says the site closed in 2025,
  so check it loads before relying on it. Its CC0 catalogue is mirrored at
  [github.com/0lhi/FreePD](https://github.com/0lhi/FreePD).
- "Bit Shift" was named earlier as an Incompetech track; I could not confirm it exists. Use
  [the Incompetech catalogue](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/music.html) and its genre
  filter rather than that title.
