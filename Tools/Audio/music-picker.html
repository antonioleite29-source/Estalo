Estalo Music Picker

Twenty-one sounds already downloaded and playable here: four music slots and the button click. Listen, pick one per slot, then copy that file into the game.

The players use 96 kbps previews. Copy the full-quality original named under each track, not the preview. Twenty of the twenty-one are CC0, so nothing to credit. Only Heart of Machine, marked in orange, needs a credit line in the Créditos panel. No chiptune left in the list.

From a pick to the game

  1. Music. Copy the original named under your pick into Assets/Resources/Music and rename it to the slot, keeping its extension: Lobby, Match, Win, Lose.
  2. Click. The game ships five tuned notes in Assets/Resources/Click — folders C, D, E, G, A, five variations each — and each button plays the note it was given. E is the neutral tap, so copying your pick over Click/E/01.wav through 05.wav changes most of the game and leaves the tuning elsewhere. Copy it into all five folders and every button sounds the same, which is the usual app behaviour but drops the pentatonic idea.
  3. Import settings: the two loops get Load Type Streaming with Vorbis; stings and clicks get Decompress On Load with ADPCM, so they start instantly. Leave Force To Mono off for music.
  4. Press Play. Music runs at volume 0.35 against 0.8 for the effects, and Som: desligado on the More page silences everything.
