# Screenshot capture list

Two uses for the same captures: the project documentation, and the Google Play store listing.
Capture everything once, at the highest resolution you have, then crop per use.

## How to capture

- **Android phone:** power + volume down. Files land in `DCIM/Screenshots`.
- **In the editor:** Game view, set the aspect to 1179x2556 (iPhone 15 Pro) and use the Game
  view's own screenshot button, or Window > General > Game, right-click the tab > Maximize, then
  take a Mac screenshot with Cmd+Shift+4 and space.
- Turn the phone to Do Not Disturb first, so no notification banner lands in the shot.
- Full battery and a clean status bar look deliberate; a half-empty battery looks like a test build.

## Google Play minimums

- Phone screenshots: between 2 and 8, each 16:9 or 9:16, shortest side at least 1080 px.
- Feature graphic: exactly 1024 x 500 px, no transparency. Required before the listing can go live.
- App icon: 512 x 512 px PNG.

## The list

Capture these in order. The first two are the ones most people will ever look at.

1. **A live 1v1 match, mid-question.** Both scores on screen, four answers visible, nothing
   pressed. This is the main store image.
2. **The moment after a correct answer.** The green button and the score going up.
3. **The solo timer running** after the opponent gets one wrong, with the ring part-filled. It
   shows the one rule that makes the game different.
4. **The learning path**, scrolled so several lesson circles and their names are visible, with
   earlier ones marked as done.
5. **A lesson teaching card**, before the questions start.
6. **The Profile page** with the stats filled in: matches, wins, losses, points, time playing,
   ranking.
7. **The lobby**, with Jogar and the mode buttons.
8. **The tutorial**, one explanation card.
9. **A 2v2 match**, four names and avatars on the board.
10. **The More page**, showing the settings buttons.

## For the report, not the store

- The login panel, empty, so the account flow is documented.
- The end of a match, with the winner message.
- The same question on a small phone and a large one, side by side, to show the text fitting.
