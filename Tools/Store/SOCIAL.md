# Social media plan

## Which network, and why

The survey asked all nine testers where they find new games. The answers:

| Where | People |
|---|---|
| Friends or school | 4 |
| App store | 3 |
| Instagram | 2 |
| TikTok | 1 |
| YouTube | 1 |
| WhatsApp | 1 |

Word of mouth beats every platform, and the store listing is second. So the plan is not "pick a
social network and post". It is: make the game easy to pass between friends, make the store page
good, and use **one** social account to feed both.

**Chosen network: Instagram**, with the same vertical videos reposted to TikTok. Instagram is where
two of the nine testers already find games, it is the network school friends in Brazil use most,
and Reels and TikTok take the same clip with no extra editing.

## The account

- Handle: `@estalo.jogo` (or `@jogoestalo` if taken).
- Bio: "Duelo de matemática em tempo real. Jogue contra seus amigos." plus the Play Store link.
- Profile picture: the app icon, so the account and the store listing match.

## What to post

Short vertical video works because the game is already a duel; the clip has a winner.

1. **Launch clip, 20 seconds.** Two phones side by side, same question, one person answers first.
   Caption: "quem responde mais rápido?"
2. **The solo rule, 15 seconds.** One player misses, the timer ring runs, the other steals the
   point. This is the rule nobody expects.
3. **The learning path, 15 seconds.** Scrolling the trail, a lesson opening, a circle turning to
   done.
4. **A real match between friends, 30 seconds**, with the score climbing to 7.
5. **One question as a still post**, asking people to answer in the comments. Cheap to make and the
   comments do the reach.

Post the first three in launch week, then roughly twice a week.

## What actually drives installs

- **A share link in the game.** Not built yet: a button that sends "joguei Estalo, me ganha?"
  with the store link. One cheap feature that turns the strongest channel (friends and school) into
  something measurable.
- **The store listing itself.** Three of nine find games by browsing the store, so the screenshots
  and short description matter more than any post.
- **School.** Ask a maths teacher to let a class try it. Nine testers came from family and friends;
  a classroom is the first honest audience.

## Timing

Start posting only when the Play Store page is public, which is late November or early December by
the plan in `PLAY_STORE.md`. Posts before that send people nowhere.
